# Easter eggs

You found the page about the hidden things. They are small, local to your browser, make no sound
and stop moving when your system asks for reduced motion.

- Press the golem on the home page. Pax says one of the lines it really says in game when it is
  idle or when someone raids its chests.
- Type `pax` anywhere outside a text field.
- Up, up, down, down, left, right, left, right, B, A.
- Click the logo in the top bar five times in a row.
- Copy a code block.
- Scroll to the end of a long page and watch the bar at the top.
- Switch to dark mode and look the golem in the eyes.
- Open the browser console.
- Visit a page that doesn't exist.

The code is in `docs/.vitepress/theme/` (`fun.ts`, `fun.css`, `HeroGolem.vue`, `PaxSays.vue`,
`FunLayer.vue`, `NotFound.vue`); the lines are in `pax-lines.ts`.
