## Requirements

### Requirement: Shore from the middle of a lake
After getting air, the drowning reflex SHALL look for dry land out to 48 blocks before giving up on getting out of the water.

#### Scenario: big lake
- **WHEN** the bot gets air 30 blocks from the nearest shore
- **THEN** it swims to that shore instead of sinking again
