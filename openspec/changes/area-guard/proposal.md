# Area guard

## Why

Owners want a bot to protect a place (the village, the farm, the fortress), not only a person.

## What changes

- An `area_guard` behaviour: patrol a named place or box, fight hostiles inside it, light up dark
  spots where mobs can spawn, close open doors at night.
- Shares threat ranking and never-attack rules with the bodyguard.

## Status

Proposed, not started.
