## Why
Live: after a death Pax respawns at world spawn, a protected area, at night. Digging in failed three times there ("protected area"), so it walked on at night with no gear and a zombie killed it, back at spawn again.

## What Changes
- A failed bunker in a protected area doesn't count towards the three tries; it walks 24 blocks on along the heading and tries again. Other failures count and move 12 blocks along the heading (was a fixed +12 +12) (`shelterFallback`).
